## 2. Stakeholder Survey Questions (Formally Structured)

Use these exact questions for Google Forms, survey questionnaires, or recorded capstone interviews:

### A. For Administrators / Program Heads / Schedulers
*(Focus: Bottlenecks, manual errors, room limits, and conflict management)*

1. **Scheduling Timeframe:** How many days or weeks does it take your department to draft, revise, and finalize a semester's timetable manually?
2. **Double-Booking & Clashes:** How frequently do room overlaps or instructor double-bookings occur during the first 2 weeks of classes?  
   *(Choices: Never | Rarely (1–2 times) | Frequently (3–5 times) | Consistently (>5 times))*
3. **Room Resource Allocation:** What are the most common challenges when distributing specialized rooms (e.g., Computer Laboratories vs. General Lecture Halls)?
4. **Irregular Student Deficiencies:** How difficult is it to track prerequisites and approve custom unit loads for irregular students under curriculum transitions?  
   *(Scale: 1 - Very Easy to 5 - Extremely Difficult/Tedious)*
5. **System Readiness:** Would an automated system that validates feasibility and detects conflicts before publishing improve departmental efficiency?  
   *(Likert Scale: Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree)*

---

### B. For Faculty / Teachers
*(Focus: Teaching loads, 7 AM–7 PM compliance, break times, and room relocations)*

1. **Working Window & Shifts:** Are your assigned classes currently distributed fairly within the university’s 07:00 AM – 07:00 PM operating window?
2. **Meal & Rest Protection:** Have you ever experienced having classes scheduled continuously through the 11:30 AM – 01:30 PM lunch break without a rest gap?  
   *(Yes / No / Occasionally)*
3. **Fatigue & Consecutive Hours:** What is your experience with consecutive teaching hours (e.g., teaching 3 or more subjects back-to-back without passing time)?
4. **Sudden Relocations:** How do you usually receive updates when a room is changed or a class is rescheduled?  
   *(Choices: Group Chat (Messenger) | Verbal notice | Formal memo | Late notice upon arrival)*
5. **Mobile Accessibility:** Would you find it beneficial to view your real-time daily schedule and check campus room vacancy directly from an authenticated mobile app?

---

### C. For Students (Regular, Irregular, and Working Students)
*(Focus: Conflict-free schedules, vacant room finder, backload clearing, and notifications)*

1. **Student Classification:** What is your current enrollment status?  
   *(Choices: Regular Block Student | Irregular Student with Backload | Working Student)*
2. **Schedule Conflicts (For Irregulars):** Have you ever experienced having a lower-year backload subject clash with a higher-year major subject on the same day and time?  
   *(Choices: Frequently | Once or twice | Never)*
3. **Room Vacancy Knowledge:** When you have vacant hours or need a place for study/group projects, how do you find available rooms on campus?  
   *(Choices: Peeking through doors | Asking guards/janitors | Roaming buildings | No way to know)*
4. **Advising & Unit Caps:** How tedious is the current paper-based advising process with the Program Head when securing slots for retakes?
5. **Real-Time Notifications:** If an administrator reschedules a class or transfers your room, would real-time mobile push notifications save you time and prevent missed classes?  
   *(Scale 1–5: 1 - Not at all, 5 - Extremely Essential)*

## 3. Post-Implementation Evaluation: ISO/IEC 25010 & System Usability Scale (SUS)

For **Chapter 4**, you will give the working Web and Mobile apps to 10–30 respondents to rate using the international standard **System Usability Scale (SUS)**:

1. *"I think that I would like to use ClassSync frequently."*
2. *"I found the system unnecessarily complex."*
3. *"I thought the system was easy to use."*
4. *"I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use ClassSync."*
5. *"I found the various functions in ClassSync were well integrated."*
6. *"I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system."*
7. *"I would imagine that most people would learn to use ClassSync very quickly."*
8. *"I found the system very cumbersome to use."*
9. *"I felt very confident using ClassSync."*
10. *"I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system."*

*(Scoring > 68 is considered Above Average; > 80 is an Excellent, High-Distinction Capstone grade).*
